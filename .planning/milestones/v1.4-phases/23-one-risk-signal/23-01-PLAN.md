<template_table>
## Per-Tier Template Table (exact strings — executor uses verbatim; owner reviews in SUMMARY)

Interpolation rule: {findingId} is finding.id and {subject} is the existing ` (title)` suffix or empty string when title is missing or empty. Templates key strictly on the tier: no branch on detection, verdict, or severity. Tier-honest framing only — no template claims a specific detection or verdict fact, since tier high serves both no-detection and violation rows and tier critical serves both undetected and fully-detected rows.

| Tier | Exact template |
|------|----------------|
| critical | `critical residual risk — {findingId}{subject} sits at the top tier; do not declare the posture acceptable while this row is critical` |
| high | `high residual risk — {findingId}{subject} sits above appetite; do not declare the posture acceptable while this row is high` |
| medium | `medium residual risk — {findingId}{subject} sits at the middle tier; harden the open items on its matrix row before declaring the posture acceptable` |
| low | `low residual risk — {findingId}{subject} sits at a low tier; confirm its matrix row before declaring the posture acceptable` |
| info | `informational residual risk — {findingId}{subject} carries no tiered risk; track its matrix row only` |

Remediation-clause note: the old covered-branch clause `pending ROE remediation of the violating action` cannot survive verbatim under strict-tier keying because tier high also serves no-detection rows with no violation; violation follow-up remains covered structurally by the unresolved membership and ROE remediation nextMissions invariants, which this phase leaves untouched.
</template_table>
